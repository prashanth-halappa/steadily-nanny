### F-CX-R15-3-1 -- L3 card body text uses wrong typography rung
**Severity:** S1
**Where:** apps/mobile/src/components/custom/CampaignCard.tsx:12
**Evidence:**       <Body className="text-muted-foreground">{announcement.body}</Body>
**Rule:** 01-LAWS.md §1 The rung model, which states that the body text for an L3 (Routine) card must use the `Small` (14px) typography token, rather than `Body`.
**What the user sees:** The announcement text appears larger than the body text of all other routine cards, distorting the intended visual hierarchy.
**Fix:** Change `<Body>` to `<Small>` in CampaignCard.tsx at the component layer.
