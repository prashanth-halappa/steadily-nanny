Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jetto LLC ("Jetto," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the Steadily Nanny mobile app, the website at nanny.getsteadily.app, and related services (together, the "Service"). It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

The Service is in beta. Features change, and errors, outages or data loss can happen. Keep your own copy of any record you need, such as hours, pay and payments. Details are in the Terms.

1. Who the Service is for

Steadily Nanny helps families and nannies or other in-home carers organize schedules, hours, pay terms and payment records for a household. You must be 18 or older to create an account. The Service is not directed to children, and children may not use it. Parents and guardians may enter information about their children (see Section 2) to arrange care.

2. Information we collect

Information you or other household members provide

  • Account and profile: your name, email address and profile picture (received from Apple or Google when you sign in), plus an optional phone number, city, country, language and time zone.
  • Household details: household name, address, time zone, currency, country and state, and an emergency contact (name, phone number and relationship).
  • Children's details entered by a parent or guardian: name, date of birth, routine notes, and school, activity and nap schedules.
  • Schedules and care records: shifts, recurring schedules, change requests, cancellations, closures, holidays, availability, time off (which can include its type, such as personal or sick leave), and handoff notes.
  • Hours and pay: clock-in and clock-out times, breaks, timesheets, pay rates and terms (for example overtime, guaranteed hours, paid time off and mileage), terms proposals and acknowledgments, expenses, reimbursements, and payment records you enter (amount, date and a note about how you paid).
  • Messages and notes: free-text notes and messages you attach to shifts, timesheets, requests, proposals and other records.
  • Invitations: invite codes, the role offered, any proposed pay offer, and the invitee's email address if you enter one.
  • Waitlist and support: your email address and whether you are a family or a nanny if you join our waitlist, and anything you send us when you contact support.

Please share only what is needed to arrange care. Do not enter government ID numbers, bank or card numbers, health records, or other sensitive information the Service does not ask for. If you enter information about another person (for example a child, co-parent, nanny or emergency contact), you confirm that you have the right to share it.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and session data: IP address, device and browser type (user agent), operating system, app version, a device identifier, and session records for keeping you signed in.
  • Push notification data: your push token, platform and notification settings.
  • Product analytics: a limited set of in-app events (for example, that a pay proposal was viewed, accepted or countered, or that a notification was opened), with standard device and app properties. These events are tied to a random identifier, not your name or email. We do not use session replay or ad trackers.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider records technical logs (such as request times, errors, account identifiers and IP addresses) to operate and secure the Service.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input. We do not use your device's advertising identifier, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Calendar and home-screen widgets

If you turn on calendar sync, the app uses the calendar permission on your device to add your shifts to your calendar, and reads back only the events it created itself. Your other calendar events are not sent to us. Home-screen widgets show schedule and hours summaries that are stored on your device.

Website

Our website does not set cookies and does not use advertising or analytics scripts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address. When someone opens a shared invite link (/t/…), we record that the link was opened and send a count, without personal profiles, to our analytics provider.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: create your account, run your household, calculate hours and pay totals from the terms you set, and show records to the right household members.
  • To send push notifications you can control in Settings, such as schedule changes, reminders, uncovered days and approvals.
  • To secure the Service: authenticate users, prevent abuse and fraud, rate-limit requests, and investigate problems.
  • To maintain and improve the Service, including understanding which features are used and fixing errors.
  • To communicate with you about the Service, the beta, your account, and support requests, and, if you joined the waitlist, about launch and availability.
  • To comply with law, enforce our Terms, and protect the rights, safety and property of users, Jetto and others.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles. We do not use the contents of your household records to train artificial-intelligence models.

4. How information is shared

With members of your household

Steadily Nanny is a shared workspace. Information in a household is visible to its other members according to their role. For example, parents and the household's nanny see the children's details, schedules, address, emergency contact and notes; a nanny's pay terms, hours, payments and time-off records are visible to that nanny and the household's parents; and names and phone numbers are visible to active members. A nanny who works for several households may have busy times shown to each household as times only, without naming the other family. Choose whom you invite carefully. Anything you add to a household can be seen, kept or copied by its members.

Through invite links

If a nanny shares an invite link for proposed terms, anyone with the link can view the nanny's first name and last initial, the proposed rate and terms, and the proposal dates, without signing in. It does not show children, surnames, addresses or contact details. Share invite links only with the intended family.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep information while your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete account. When you do:

  • Your account, profile, sign-in records, devices and push tokens, notification settings, household memberships, availability and time off are deleted.
  • Households with no remaining members are deleted along with everything in them.
  • Shared household records are kept for the remaining members. Shifts, timesheets, time entries, payments, expenses, pay terms and notes you took part in stay in that household's history, because other members rely on them (for example, a family's record of hours and payments). They are no longer linked to your account but can still show the name and content recorded at the time.
  • Deleted data may remain in backups for up to about 30 days, and in logs for a limited time, before it is overwritten.

To remove your email from the waitlist, or to ask us to delete other information, email ops@jettohq.com. We may keep information where the law requires it, to resolve disputes, to prevent fraud or abuse, or to enforce our agreements.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls and role-based permissions. No system is perfectly secure, and especially during the beta we cannot guarantee the security of information. Keep your device and your Apple or Google account secure, and tell us right away at ops@jettohq.com if you suspect unauthorized access.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is for adults. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. Information about children is entered by their parent or guardian, or by a carer acting for the household, only to arrange that child's care, and is visible only to that household's members. A parent or guardian can review, change or delete a child's information in the app, or by contacting us. If you believe a child has given us information directly, contact us and we will delete it.

  • Deletion: delete your account in Settings, or contact us.
  • Notifications: turn off push notifications in the app or your device settings.
  • Calendar: turn off calendar sync, or revoke calendar access in your device settings.

If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.
