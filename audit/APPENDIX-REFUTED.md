### F-B2-2 — REFUTED
**Verdict basis:** `weekEarningsService.ts:145-151`, `:271-286`, `:317-321`; `earningsService.ts:450-451`, `:605-612`, `:747-758`; `timesheetCommandService.ts:245-293`; `docs/11-MONEY.md` §5/§7; `earningsService.test.ts:772-788`.
**If REFUTED:** The cited netting (`weekEarningsService.ts:145-151`) fixes **cancelled-then-worked** (usage + reversing `adjustment` on the same `time_off_id`), documented in `docs/11-MONEY.md:276-278`. It does not claim mutual exclusion for confirmed time off + worked hours. Product rule: `payable_minutes = worked + cancellation_paid + PTO usage` (`docs/11-MONEY.md:329-332`, `TIER0-PLAN.md:491`). `computeWeekEarnings` independently sums `workedByDate` and `ptoByDate` (`earningsService.ts:450-451`, `:605-612`); `earningsService.test.ts:772-788` asserts additive gross (`29600` for 480+480). Same-day Mon PTO 480 + Mon worked 480 → `pto` `14800` + `regular` `14800` = `29600` by spec, not omission. Finder’s “expected one line” is not a product invariant.

### F-B4-4 — REFUTED
